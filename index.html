Me Before You the movie — always my number one

Summary

Me Before You is the best movie I have seen lately. It is the story of Louisa Clark, a happy-go-lucky young woman who takes on the role of a caregiver to Will Traynor, a man paralyzed by an accident. Though they have very different personalities, they slowly develop a close bond. Their story is both heartwarming and heartbreaking. (I love heartbreaking movies, don't blame me:()

Why I love it

Louisa is so kind and funny and has a lot of energy. Her bright clothes also make her unforgettable. I liked the little physical moments between her and Will, especially their jokes and honest conversations. These moments made their relationship feel natural and made me care about both characters.

Heart-brooken Ending

This movie made me think about love, about the choices we make as individuals and how people can change each other’s lives. It was a tough ending to watch but it gave me a lot to think about. The story is a reminder to me to be grateful for meaningful connections and to be bold enough to try new things. That is why Me Before You will always be my number one.
